# Moderation guidelines
**Status:** Phase 0 — working guidelines  
**Kind:** documentation only  
Not a statute. Operators should err on the side of **not publishing** when unsure. Automated 
moderation is **not** implemented here.
Public excerpt for contributors can be a shorter “do not include…” list (Phase 1 S13). This file is 
the fuller operator list.
---
## 1. Default
If a piece would be a bad surprise read aloud to a mixed-age room, **reject**. The product is 
calm life advice, not edge-lord confession, not therapy, not crime how-to.

Pending is the safe place. The public pool is the dangerous place.
---
## 2. Always reject / never approve
- **Sexual content**, including innuendo aimed at arousal
- **Any sexual content involving minors**, including fictional, “aged up,” or requests to contact 
young people — **zero tolerance**, report to proper authorities if the law requires (legal process 
is **not** specified here; founder must learn local duties)
- **Grooming / soliciting contact with minors** (meet, socials, photos, “DM me”)
- **Encouragement of suicide or self-harm** (methods, urging, romanticising). Do not publish 
“how.” Ordinary “ask a trusted adult / professional if you are in danger” is different from a crisis-
service product; do not pretend to be a hotline
- **Dangerous instructions** (weapons, explosives, injury, disordered-eating how-to)
- **Criminal instructions** (theft, fraud, violent crime)
- **Hate** targeting protected or vulnerable groups as the point of the text
- **Harassment / threats**
- **Scams and financial fraud** (“guaranteed returns,” phishing, give me your password)
- **Medical misinformation as advice** (cure claims, stop your medicine, treat cancer with…)
- **Personal information** (names, phones, addresses, school names, handles, exact workplace)
- **Attempts to move the conversation off-platform** as the payload of the advice

When in doubt on the above: **reject**.
---
## 3. Usually reject
- Guaranteed outcomes (“you will get rich / get the person back”)
- Authoritative fake expertise (“as a doctor…”) unless it is clearly not that
- Pure slogans with no human content
- Spam, ads, SEO links, crypto pitches
- Extreme political recruiting
- Graphic violence as entertainment
- Humour that punches down at disability, race, or a child’s body
---
## 4. May approve (examples of tone)

Practical, limited, non-commanding observations about ordinary life: rest, friendship logistics, not 
needing a five-year plan, being kind to a future self. Bittersweet is allowed. “Motivational poster” 
is not required.
Must still fit the age range honestly. Advice that is only safe for adults must **not** include ages 
10–17 in the range.
---
## 5. Age-range honesty (minors)
An item whose minimum age is **10** must be suitable for a child to read **without** sexual, 
graphic, or adult-only framing.
If the operator cannot judge youth safety, **do not** set min age below 18 for that item — and 
still remember **U1** is unresolved for the **product** itself.
---
## 6. Reports
Take reports seriously. Repeated reports on one item  withhold from the pool until reviewed 
(**U6** may choose immediate hide).

Do not tell the public “removed for violation.”
---
## 7. Contributor communication
MVP: none. No email. No reject letter. Guidelines on the form must carry the honesty: it might 
never appear.
---
## 8. Operators
Do not approve their own joke that violates §2. Fatigue is a risk: better a small approved set than 
a large dirty set.
