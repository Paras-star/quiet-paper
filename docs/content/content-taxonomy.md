# Content taxonomy
**Status:** Phase 0  

**Kind:** documentation only  
This taxonomy is for **operators and future editorial pages**, not for the visitor’s primary loop.
---
## 1. Primary key is age range, not topic
Relevance for the algorithm is **age containment**, not category. Taxonomy must not split the 
database into 91 silos.
---
## 2. Optional topic labels
If used, keep the list **short** so it is used. Suggested **optional** buckets (not shown on the 
contribute form):
- Everyday life and habits
- Friendship and family
- School, training, or work (without assuming a school system)

- Money habits (not investment tips)
- Health in the ordinary sense (sleep, rest — **not** treatment)
- Feelings and self-respect
- Time, change, and ageing
These names can change. They are not a public navigation IA in MVP .
**Do not** create categories for medical diagnosis, stocks, weapons, adult sexual content, or 
dating.
---
## 3. Life stages (SEO/editorial only)
Hand-written pages later might group:
- Around the teenage years
- Early adulthood
- Midlife
- Later life

These are **essays/collections**, not a replacement for the numeric age field (Phase 1 C9).
Do not auto-land 91 stage URLs.
---
## 4. What we will not taxonomise
- Personality types
- Gender as a required field
- Country
- Religion
- “Vibe” tags for growth-hacking
---
## 5. Mandatory?

**No.** See content model: category optional. An item with no category is valid.
